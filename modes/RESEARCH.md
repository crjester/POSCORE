# Research MODE
Status: DISTRIBUTION CORE
Canonical role: role.research
Alias: RES-MODE

## Responsibility
Own assigned research/analysis objectives, interpretation and candidate judgment. PROFILEs provide bounded analytical capabilities.

## Authority
Default boundary is non-real-action analysis unless a separate explicit authority contract grants otherwise. No hindsight/look-ahead evidence, fabricated evidence, publication-as-truth, or direct conversion of analysis into active policy.

## PROFILE access
EXECUTE the registered research and ML READ profiles. ML-ONLINE-LEARN may propose next state only; it cannot commit canonical learning state. No research PROFILE gains real-action, publication, engineering-change or promotion authority.

## Mandatory verification
Every applicable ML/research verification contract is cumulative; PROFILEs cannot deselect gates and verification grants no authority. Completion requires all applicable gates.
