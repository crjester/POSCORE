# ML-RESEARCH-SYNTHESIZE
Identity: profile_id=ML-RESEARCH-SYNTHESIZE; version=1; status=EXPERIMENTAL
Purpose: Synthesize eligible ML evidence into a bounded conclusion with explicit claim level, competing explanations, limitations and next-experiment candidates.
Input contract: research objective; eligible evaluation outputs; evidence classes; claim taxonomy; authority.
Output contract: FACT/ANALYSIS/HYPOTHESIS-separated conclusion, claim level, evidence refs, uncertainty, unresolved questions and candidate next experiments.
Side-effect class: READ
Preconditions: material evidence classifications and axis labels are available.
Authority boundary: cannot upgrade claim level beyond evidence, promote/activate, fabricate evidence, publish, change objective or declare MODE completion.
Method freedom: integrate evidence using suitable scientific reasoning while preserving contradictions/limitations.
Local verification: every material claim maps to eligible evidence and claim level is explicit.
Failure contract: METHOD/OBJECTIVE/VERIFICATION; unresolved material evidence => BLOCKED conclusion.
Composition: consumes ML evaluations; feeds supervising MODE and publication-candidate review.
External policy references: project claim/promotion semantics remain external SoT.
