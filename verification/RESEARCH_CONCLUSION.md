Identity: verification_id=VERIFY-RESEARCH-CONCLUSION; version=1; status=EXPERIMENTAL
Claim / gate: bounded research conclusion is supportable.
Applicability trigger: TL presents a research/analysis conclusion as complete.
Required evidence: eligible supporting evidence; material counterevidence/limitations; reproducibility refs; FACT/ANALYSIS/HYPOTHESIS separation.

Evaluation rule: PASS when claim strength matches eligible evidence; FAIL when claim exceeds/contradicts evidence; BLOCKED when material evidence validity unresolved.
Output: standard verification envelope.
Lifecycle effect: PASS permits TL to accept the bounded research conclusion.
