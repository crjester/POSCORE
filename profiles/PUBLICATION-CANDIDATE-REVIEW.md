# PUBLICATION-CANDIDATE-REVIEW
Identity: profile_id=PUBLICATION-CANDIDATE-REVIEW; version=1; status=EXPERIMENTAL
Purpose: Assess research material as Reject, Merge, Defer or Publish candidate without publishing or altering research truth.
Input contract: research conclusion/evidence refs, publication criteria, cutoff/disclosure constraints, authority.
Output contract: candidate disposition recommendation, rationale, evidence refs, uncertainty.
Side-effect class: READ
Preconditions: research material sufficiently identified.
Authority boundary: cannot publish, modify canonical research state, activate strategy/tuning, or declare completion.
Method freedom: compare publication value/readiness within criteria.
Local verification: recommendation preserves FACT/ANALYSIS/HYPOTHESIS and evidence/cutoff refs.
Failure contract: METHOD/AUTHORITY/VERIFICATION.
Composition: feeds TL decision and possible TRANSFER package to TD.
External policy references: research publication criteria/publication service policy.
