# RESEARCH-SYNTHESIZE
Identity: profile_id=RESEARCH-SYNTHESIZE; version=1; status=EXPERIMENTAL
Purpose: Synthesize eligible research evidence into a bounded conclusion separating FACT, ANALYSIS and HYPOTHESIS.
Input contract: objective, eligible evidence/results, constraints, cutoff, authority.
Output contract: conclusion, FACT/ANALYSIS/HYPOTHESIS separation, evidence refs, uncertainty, unresolved questions.
Side-effect class: READ
Preconditions: material evidence classifications available.
Authority boundary: cannot promote/activate, fabricate evidence, convert fitted performance into predictive proof, publish, or declare MODE completion.
Method freedom: integrate evidence and competing explanations within objective.
Local verification: material claims map to eligible evidence and epistemic labels are explicit.
Failure contract: METHOD/AUTHORITY/OBJECTIVE/VERIFICATION.
Composition: consumes research profiles; feeds TL judgment/publication candidate review.
External policy references: research/project contracts.
