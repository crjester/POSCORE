# VERIFY-ML-ROBUSTNESS
Identity: verification_id=VERIFY-ML-ROBUSTNESS; version=1; status=EXPERIMENTAL
Claim / gate: evidence breadth/stability is sufficient for the stated generalization.
Applicability trigger: robust/generalizable/stable claim or promotion consideration.
Required evidence: claim-proportionate chronological OOS/holdout or prospective evidence; fold/regime stability; parameter/sensitivity evidence where material; risk/cost/tail behavior where material; coverage and evidence-authority dependence. Missing a mandatory dimension => BLOCKED.
Invariants: raw return, fitted performance or one favorable fold cannot alone support a broad generalization; incompatible evidence is not silently pooled.
Evaluation rule: PASS when predeclared robustness requirements support claim scope; FAIL when evidence demonstrates material instability contradicting claim; BLOCKED when required breadth is unavailable.
Output: standard verification envelope.
Lifecycle effect: PASS permits the stated robustness level, not a stronger claim.
