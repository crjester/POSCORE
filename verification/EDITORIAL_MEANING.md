# VERIFY-EDITORIAL-MEANING
Identity: verification_id=VERIFY-EDITORIAL-MEANING; version=1; status=EXPERIMENTAL
Claim / gate: editorial artifact preserves authorized meaning.
Applicability trigger: any TD artifact presented as finished editorial/content.
Required evidence: authoritative source/meaning refs; artifact; material claim/source mapping; inclusion/exclusion/disclosure constraints. Missing authoritative meaning => BLOCKED.
Invariants: no unsupported domain conclusion; no silent promotion to project truth; sourced fact vs interpretation distinguishable where material.
Evaluation rule: PASS iff meaning/constraints preserved; FAIL on contradiction/invention/material omission; BLOCKED when source meaning cannot be resolved.
Output: standard verification envelope.
Lifecycle effect: PASS permits editorial acceptance subject to other applicable gates.
